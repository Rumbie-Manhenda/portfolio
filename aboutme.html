Journey so far

I started out sceptical about technology. That quote changed the direction of my life.

I was uneasy about what technology might do to morality and to people. Reading Omar Bradley made me see the other side of the argument: that technology is a force for good, but only when the people building it bring judgement with them. I decided to be one of those people.

Driven by curiosity, I went into Computer Science — and did well, exploring front-end web development and UX/UI design along the way. It was not until I met artificial intelligence that I found the thing I actually wanted to spend a career on.

The path was not smooth. I struggled with my mental health along the way, and found strength in volunteering and in poetry. Scholarships — the Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics scholarship programme, and the Varadi Scholarship — made higher education possible, and I earned my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science.

Fueled by a thirst for knowledge and a wish to leave things better than I found them, the journey continues.
