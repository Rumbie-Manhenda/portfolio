Dear Hiring Manager,

I’ve always been curious about technology, but what interests me most is understanding how things work, why they work, and how we can use them to solve meaningful problems. That curiosity led me to study Computer Science, where I graduated with First-Class Honours, specialising in Machine Learning and Neural Networks. It is also what continues to shape the kind of engineer I want to become.

Since November 2024, I’ve been working as a NOC Engineer at Optasia. Working in a technical operations environment has given me a different perspective on technology beyond writing code. It has strengthened my appreciation for the systems, reliability and problem-solving that keep technology working in the real world. It has also reinforced my desire to grow further into software development and machine learning, where I can contribute more directly to building and improving the systems themselves.

Alongside work, I’m pursuing a master’s degree in Science, Technology and Society at the University of Athens. I chose this path because I believe understanding technology requires more than understanding the technical side. I’m interested in the decisions behind what we build, the people affected by those decisions, and the responsibility that comes with creating increasingly powerful technologies. For me, this perspective is particularly important in AI and machine learning.

My background includes Python, machine learning frameworks, backend development and hands-on projects that have allowed me to explore ideas through code. I enjoy the process of taking a problem, breaking it down, experimenting with possible solutions and learning from what does and doesn’t work. I’m also making a conscious effort to deepen my understanding of the mathematics and reasoning behind machine learning, rather than simply learning how to use the tools.

I’m looking for an opportunity where I can contribute what I already know while continuing to grow alongside people who are good at what they do. I bring curiosity, determination and a willingness to take initiative, but I also value collaboration, mentorship and being challenged to think differently. I enjoy documenting what I learn and simplifying processes so that the knowledge gained can be useful to others, too.

I’m particularly interested in machine learning, AI engineering and software development because I want to be involved in building technology that is not only technically sound but also useful and thoughtfully designed. I may not have all the answers yet, but I bring a genuine desire to learn, the discipline to keep improving and the willingness to put in the work.

Furthermore, I hold a valid work permit for Greece and am open to relocation opportunities with visa sponsorship. I am excited about the possibility of contributing to your team, and I am confident that my skills, experience and enthusiasm make me a strong candidate for a machine learning or AI engineering role.

Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to the opportunity to discuss how my background, skills, and enthusiasms align with the needs of your organisation. Please feel free to contact me at your convenience.

Warm regards, Patricia Manhenda
